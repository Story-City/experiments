The Decryptor

The letter comes back scrambled. Each swatch points at a colour in it. Tap one, then photograph something near you that matches it, and that part of the picture comes back.

  • The camera screen is the colour, except a rounded window in the middle. Fill the window with something that matches, then snap.
  • To pass, the photo has to be in the same colour family. Each match also gets an accuracy score to brag about.
  • Finish all three to get a match card to share, and the score is saved to the story history.
  • On a laptop the camera is your webcam.
