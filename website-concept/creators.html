How a story is built

Chapters, choices and places.

A Story City story is a branching path. Each chapter happens somewhere, and each choice decides where the player walks next.

Chapters

Short scenes told in chat bubbles. Characters message the player, and the story waits until they arrive.

Choices

Players pick what happens. Choices branch the story and can lead to different endings. They never cost steps.

Places

Give each location a name and place tags, like park or café. Play Anywhere stories auto-place on any player's own streets.

Map modes

Each chapter can suit Walk, Path or Hunt. Players can switch modes any time.

Click a choice to follow a branch

  • Walk: steps unlock the next chapter
  • Path: walk to a real place
  • Hunt: find it, photograph it

Can I write for my own city?

Yes. A Local story is written for specific places in one city. A Play Anywhere story uses place tags, so it can be played on anyone's streets.

How do I pick my story's colour?

Each story picks one accent from the jewel set. It colours the buttons, choices and progress inside your story. Map mode colours are kept for the map.
